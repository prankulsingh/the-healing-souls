About

Professional support that still feels human.

Vishakha Singh is a Counselling Psychologist creating a warm, non-judgmental space for reflection, emotional clarity, and gentle change.

Not crisis care. This website is not for emergencies or crisis care. If you feel at risk of harming yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately. Tele-MANAS India: 14416.

Credentials

  • B.A. (Hons) Psychology — Delhi University
  • Master’s in Applied Psychology — Amity University
  • Certified in CBT
  • Certified in REBT
  • Pursuing Diploma in Guidance and Counselling — Jamia Millia Islamia (Open)
  • Diploma in Elementary Education

Modalities

  • Client-Centered Therapy
  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
  • Rational Emotive Behaviour Therapy (REBT)
  • Mindfulness-Based Techniques
  • Eclectic Approach

Who this space is for

People moving through overwhelm, overthinking, burnout, heartbreak, boundary struggles, low self-worth, or a desire to understand themselves more clearly.

This website offers educational and reflective support. It does not replace emergency care, diagnosis, or crisis intervention.
