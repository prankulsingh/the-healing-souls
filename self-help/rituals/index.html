Rituals

Short routines for hard and ordinary days.

Small practices can create a sense of rhythm without turning healing into another task list.

Not crisis care. This website is not for emergencies or crisis care. If you feel at risk of harming yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately. Tele-MANAS India: 14416.

Morning: one breath, one glass of water, one sentence of intention.

Midday: unclench your jaw, stretch your hands, choose one next task.

Evening: dim the lights, name what you carried, name what can wait.

Hard day: lower the standard, ask for support, return to basics.
