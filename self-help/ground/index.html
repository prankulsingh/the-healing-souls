Grounding

5-4-3-2-1 grounding room

Move through the prompts slowly. There is no score, result, or diagnosis.

Not crisis care. This website is not for emergencies or crisis care. If you feel at risk of harming yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately. Tele-MANAS India: 14416.

Step 1

Name 5 things you can see.

Step 2

Notice 4 things you can feel.

Step 3

Listen for 3 things you can hear.

Step 4

Find 2 things you can smell.

Step 5

Name 1 thing you can taste or one kind thing you can say to yourself.
